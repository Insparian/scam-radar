# Code of Conduct

Scam Radar exists to help people recognize harmful manipulation without blaming or exposing victims. Participation in this project must follow the same standard.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss people and disagreements respectfully.
- Critique evidence, wording, policy, and code rather than attacking a person.
- Protect victim privacy. Use synthetic examples unless public, necessary evidence has been reviewed for inclusion.
- State uncertainty honestly and distinguish fact, inference, and preference.
- Accept correction and help make the project understandable to non-specialists.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, or publishing another person's private information.
- Victim-blaming, ridicule, or using real victim details to make an example more dramatic.
- Unsupported public accusations against a person, company, product, or organization.
- Submitting secrets, live credentials, malicious payloads, raw production data, or private reviewer material.
- Deliberately weakening evidence, publication, authentication, or audit controls.

## Reporting

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately to `rui@insparian.com`. Do not open a public issue when the report contains personal data, an exploitable vulnerability, credentials, or an unpublished allegation.

The project maintainer may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect people or the integrity of the project. Reports will be handled as privately and proportionately as practical.
